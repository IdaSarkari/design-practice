# Perenula

Perenual apps provide a convenient way to monitor and track the health of your plants. They offer features like watering schedules, fertilizing reminders, and even a visual timeline of your plant’s growth.

## Problem

Users may have difficulty accurately identifying their plants, which can lead to incorrect care instructions.
Users may have plants with unique care requirements that are not covered by generic instructions.
Users may encounter incomplete or inconsistent information about plant care, leading to confusion.
Users may struggle to keep track of when they last watered, fertilized, or repotted their plants.

## Solution

Implement a powerful plant identification feature using image recognition technology or user-input descriptions. Utilize a comprehensive plant database to improve accuracy.
Allow users to create custom plant profiles where they can input specific care requirements and receive personalized recommendations.
Include features for users to log their care activities, such as watering, pruning, and fertilizing, with optional reminders.
